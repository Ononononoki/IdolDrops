# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Fixed missing idols when burning, poison or spirit damage made the kill. Such kills now count when a player hit the enemy.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Enemies drop Forge of Potential idols of their own biome. Tougher enemies and stars raise the chance.
- Bosses and minibosses always drop one.
- No idols from tamed creatures, summons or animals. Only player kills count.
- Enemies from other creature mods are added on their own.
- Config synced from the server.
